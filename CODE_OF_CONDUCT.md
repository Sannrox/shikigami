# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in `shikigami` to be a respectful, harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level,
or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Assume good intent while still addressing harmful impact.
- Respect privacy, consent, and requests to stop an unwanted interaction.
- Help make technical discussions accessible to newcomers.
- Disclose conflicts of interest that could affect a project decision.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Personal attacks, insults, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately exposing credentials, private data, or vulnerability details.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and
other project spaces. It also applies when someone officially represents the
project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner, `@Sannrox`, using a
private contact method listed on their GitHub profile. Do not open a public
issue that identifies the people involved. Use the private security reporting
process only when a report also involves an exploitable vulnerability or
sensitive security data ([SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).

The maintainer will review reports as confidentially and promptly as practical,
avoid conflicts of interest, and may remove content, reject contributions,
issue a warning, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. The
maintainer will consider context, impact, pattern, and the safety of affected
participants when choosing a response.

Good-faith reports will not result in retaliation. Knowingly false or malicious
reports may themselves violate this code.
